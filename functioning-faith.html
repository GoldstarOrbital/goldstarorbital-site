What is Functioning Faith for?

Functioning Faith brings Christian community and everyday growth into one experience. The product combines a social feed, groups, a prayer wall, and fitness paired with scripture. Its focus is a rhythm of connection, prayer, and activity, with ways to participate beyond simply reading a daily message.

Connect with community

Explore the feed and groups as places to take part in conversation and shared interests.

Make space for prayer

The prayer wall gives prayer a place within the community experience.

Bring activity into your routine

Fitness tracking paired with scripture connects physical activity with the app's faith-centered purpose.

The screenshots show the product experience presented on this site. As beta development continues, screens and available features can change. The app itself is the best place to see the current version.

How do I get Functioning Faith on iPhone?

  1. Open the official Functioning Faith TestFlight invitation on your iPhone.
  2. Follow Apple's prompts to install TestFlight if needed.
  3. Use the invitation to install the available Functioning Faith beta.

TestFlight displays current availability, supported devices, and installation requirements. This is beta access through Apple's testing service; it is not an App Store release announcement. If the invitation is unavailable, use the web demo or contact us for the current access route.

Can I explore it before installing the beta?

Yes. The Functioning Faith web demo is linked alongside the iOS beta. It provides another way to explore the product. Web and beta releases can differ, so check the version you are using when reporting feedback.

Who is behind Functioning Faith?

Functioning Faith is a Goldstar Orbital venture. Learn about the company and its founder, Alexander Marcus Goldsmith, or contact the team about access and feedback. Useful feedback includes the screen, the device, and the steps that led to the issue.

Questions answered

How do I join the Functioning Faith iOS beta?

Open the official TestFlight invitation at https://testflight.apple.com/join/SjmBBQbu on your iPhone and follow Apple's installation instructions. TestFlight shows the current availability and device requirements.

Is the TestFlight link an App Store release?

No. This link is an invitation to an iOS beta distributed through Apple's TestFlight service.

Can I explore Functioning Faith on the web?

Yes. The Functioning Faith web demo is linked from this page as an alternative way to explore the experience.
