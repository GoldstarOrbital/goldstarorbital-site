How to play

Best played on a desktop or laptop with a keyboard and a WebGL-capable browser. Basic touch controls are also available. Open the three-bar menu for the complete controls, skins, sensitivity, sound, save/load, and restart.

Your device. Your expedition.

No sign-up, game server, or database. Your callsign, high score, preferences, and saved flight stay in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data removes those saves. Saves from the local development version do not transfer automatically to the website.
