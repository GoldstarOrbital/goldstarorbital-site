Who founded Goldstar Orbital?

Alexander Marcus Goldsmith is the founder and CEO of Goldstar Orbital, Inc. The company brings together digital products and a longer-term interest in infrastructure beyond Earth. You can find the founder on LinkedIn and the company's code presence on GitHub.

What does Goldstar Orbital work on?

  • Fawn is a financial technology venture designed around campus life.
  • Functioning Faith is a Christian community app with prayer, groups, and fitness paired with scripture. Its iOS beta invitation and web demo are available on the product page.
  • Goldstar Orbital's photonic infrastructure research explores a hybrid accelerator, spacecraft systems, and a possible satellite-assisted photosynthesis research track.

These projects are at different stages. Product demonstrations and beta invitations describe ways to explore software. The orbital diagrams describe a proposed architecture and do not establish flight-qualified hardware or measured spacecraft performance.

How are the research pages prepared?

Goldstar Orbital publishes the guides as explanations of its own concept drawings and engineering decisions. The content builds on the Revision B design review and links to primary sources where it discusses outside research. The guides are published by the company; the byline does not imply an independent peer review.

We distinguish four kinds of statement: a result reported by an external source, a calculation with stated assumptions, a proposed design choice, and an open research question. That distinction helps a reader see what evidence supports a claim and what a future experiment must still measure.

Dates on guide pages identify their publication or substantive revision. Original drawings remain accessible in the architecture page's historical-reference section. A supplier named in an old concept drawing should not be read as a verified commercial relationship.

How do I ask a question or report a correction?

Email alexgoldsmith@goldstarorbital.com. Include the page, the statement or diagram, and any supporting evidence. For software feedback, mention the product, device, and steps involved. The goal is to keep the published explanation useful as the work develops.
