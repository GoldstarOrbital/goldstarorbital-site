What is Fawn?

Fawn is the financial technology venture in Goldstar Orbital's portfolio. It is designed around campus life and a fresh approach to everyday money. This page identifies the venture and connects visitors to its product website, where the experience and current product information live.

Fawn, Functioning Faith, and the orbital research program address different needs. Fawn focuses on the financial technology experience; Functioning Faith centers on Christian community, prayer, and activity; the orbital architecture is a research concept. A claim about one venture does not describe the others.
