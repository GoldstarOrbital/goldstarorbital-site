Golden Orbital Defense

PLAY IN YOUR BROWSER ↗

Start on Earth in pixel art. Dodge birds and satellites, dock at the ISS, then take command of a 3D spacecraft on a four-minute journey to Mars. Fight unpredictable asteroid attacks, deliver your colonist, and transform your ship into an orbital defender.

Launch. Dock. Explore.

A ten-second ascent, precision docking, and third-person flight with threats approaching from every direction.

Build your defenses.

Earn points for distance and asteroid destruction. Call in reserve rockets, dock for weapon upgrades, and deploy solar arrays.

Keep Mars growing.

Collect reactor cores and send photon energy to the surface. Protect your colony, warm its greenhouses, and grow crops.

How to play

Best played on a desktop or laptop with a keyboard and a WebGL-capable browser. Basic touch controls are also available. Open the three-bar menu for the complete controls, skins, sensitivity, sound, save/load, and restart.

Flight essentials
ActionControls
Steer / maneuverArrow keys and WASD; Q/E to roll in space
Dock at the ISSMatch position and roll, use Shift for fine thrust, then Enter to latch
Fire photons / toggle unlocked auto-fireSpace / F

Intercept / collect cores / warm colonyC / G / H

Your device. Your expedition.

No sign-up, game server, or database. Your callsign, high score, preferences, and saved flight stay in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data removes those saves. Saves from the local development version do not transfer automatically to the website.
