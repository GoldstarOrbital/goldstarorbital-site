Golden Orbital Defense

PLAY IN YOUR BROWSER

You start on the launch pad in pixel art and climb through ten seconds of birds and satellites. Above the atmosphere the view opens up into 3D. You fly the docking approach to the International Space Station, take on fuel, and set out for Mars. The crossing takes about four minutes and gets harder the closer you get.

At Mars the crew lands and the job changes. Asteroids come in from every direction, aimed at the colony, and they get better at slipping past you. Between waves you put satellites into orbit to help hold the line. Chain kills to build your multiplier, earn a six-second Overdrive burst, and face an armoured Titan on every fifth wave.

Eight levels to Mars orbit

Liftoff, the ISS rendezvous, five stretches of open space, then Mars. The levels after that keep going for as long as the colony holds.

Real spacecraft

The station, your orbiter and the satellites you build use NASA's public 3D models of the ISS, the Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter, TDRS, Landsat 8 and others. Most asteroids use the shape model of Bennu; Titans carry distinctive glowing armour.

A colony to keep alive

Keep the greenhouses warm, bring out specialists, and once you have caught enough reactor cores, detonate warheads on the polar ice to thicken the air.

How to play

It plays best on a desktop or laptop with a keyboard and mouse. Touch controls cover the basics. The in-game menu has the full control list, sensitivity, sound and saves.

SteerWASD or arrow keys
AimMouse. Click the game to take control, Esc to let go.

Saves

There is no account and no game server. Your callsign, best score and saved flight live in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data removes them, so the menu lets you download a backup file and load it again later or on another computer.
