Flight controls

Ascent: WASD / arrows to dodge.
Dock: WASD / arrows to align, Q/E to match roll, Shift for fine thrust/braking. Settle for 0.8 seconds, then Space (or Enter) during green capture window.
Space: arrows aim (pitch/yaw); WASD moves (W faster, S slower, A/D strafe); Space fires and latches when docking; Shift gives fine control. Let go of the arrows and the ship steers itself back toward Mars. Hold B to look back; red edge markers show where asteroids are coming from. At Mars, WASD moves around the planet and E deploys repair robots. Upgrades and crew tools are buttons in this menu. P pauses.
